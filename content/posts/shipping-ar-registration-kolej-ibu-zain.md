title: "Shipping AR registration for Kolej Ibu Zain"
date: "2026-09-18"
tags: [AR, mobile, campus]
---

Kolej Ibu Zain needed a clearer path through student registration. We built the KIZ Super App for that, and the AR feature is now live on campus.

Registration is usually a stack of forms, queues, and "go to this counter next." AR lets students point their phone at the right place in the physical flow and get guided through what to do next, instead of guessing from a PDF or a pinned notice.

I kept the scope tight on purpose. One campus workflow. One feature that has to work in a real hallway, not a demo. Finish it, put it in students' hands, then iterate from what breaks.

What's next is less about new features and more about watching how registration actually runs with AR in the loop.
